❓ FAQ

Common questions

Is UpKlick really free to start?
+
Yes — the Starter plan is free forever with no credit card required. You get full dashboard access, AI assistant (50 msgs/mo), CRM, content tools, and more. Upgrade to Pro when you want unlimited AI and marketing tools.
Is it available in Arabic?
+
نعم بالكامل. UpKlick يدعم العربية بالكامل — الواجهة، الـ AI، والمحتوى. يمكنك التبديل بين العربية والإنجليزية في أي وقت من الإعدادات. الـ AI مدرب خصيصاً للسوق العربي.
Can I replace tools like Notion, Trello, and Mailchimp?
+
Most people who use UpKlick cancel 4-7 other subscriptions. It covers CRM (replaces HubSpot), task management (replaces Trello/Notion), basic email marketing (replaces Mailchimp for simple campaigns), content planning, and financial tracking. We integrate with tools you want to keep.
Which payment methods work for Arab markets?
+
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Tap Payments (MADA, KNET, STC Pay), PayPal, and bank transfer. Invoices available in AED, SAR, KWD, EGP, and USD.
How is this different from other all-in-one tools?
+
UpKlick is the only platform built specifically for Arab entrepreneurs and creators — with Arabic-first AI, Arab market data (WhatsApp-centric sales, Arab e-commerce integrations), and cultural context baked in. It's not a translated Western tool.
What happens when my free trial ends?
+
You automatically drop to the free Starter plan — your data is safe and you keep access to core features. No surprise charges. Upgrade anytime to restore Pro features.
